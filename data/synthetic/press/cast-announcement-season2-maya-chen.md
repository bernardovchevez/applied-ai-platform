Harbor Line Season 2 Adds Maya Chen as Series Regular

NEW YORK — July 20, 2026 — Harbor Media Group announced that **Maya Chen** will join the cast of **Harbor Line** as Lia Park, a union organizer who arrives at the East River docks as labor tensions escalate in Season 2.

Chen, known for her work in independent drama, will appear in all eight Season 2 episodes. She begins filming in April 2027.

"Lia Park changes the balance of power on the waterfront," said showrunner T. Whitfield. Season 2 premieres **March 22, 2027** on Harbor Stream.

Cast is subject to standard talent NDAs until promotional cycle opens.
