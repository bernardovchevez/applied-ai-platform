Harbor Media Group Renews Acclaimed Drama "Harbor Line" for Season 2

NEW YORK — July 15, 2026 — Harbor Media Group announced today that it has ordered Season 2 of the hit drama series **Harbor Line**, produced by Harbor Street Productions. The renewal follows a strong Season 1 performance on the company's streaming platform.

**Season 2 premiere date: March 22, 2027** — same window as the Season 1 finale broadcast event.

"We're thrilled to continue the story of the dockworkers and families who built this city," said Jamie Martinez, Executive Producer. Season 2 will expand to eight episodes and introduce new locations along the East River corridor.

Season 1 remains available on Harbor Stream. International distribution partners will be announced at MIPCOM.
